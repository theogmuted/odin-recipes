Cacio e Pepe

This classic Roman pasta dish features a simple yet delicious combination of Pecorino Romano cheese and black pepper.

Ingredients:

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 100g Pecorino Romano cheese, grated
  • 2 teaspoons freshly ground black pepper
  • Salt, to taste

Instructions:

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the spaghetti according to package instructions until al dente.
  2. While the pasta is cooking, toast the black pepper in a large skillet over medium heat for about 1 minute, until fragrant.
  3. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain the spaghetti and add it to the skillet with the toasted black pepper.
  4. Reduce the heat to low and gradually add the grated Pecorino Romano cheese, tossing the pasta to create a creamy sauce. If the sauce is too thick, add a little reserved pasta water until you reach the desired consistency.
  5. Season with salt to taste and serve immediately, garnished with extra Pecorino Romano cheese and a sprinkle of black pepper.
