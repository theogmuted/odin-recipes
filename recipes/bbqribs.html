LOW AND SLOW BBQ RIBS

These ribs are cooked low and slow to perfection, resulting in tender, flavorful meat that falls off the bone. The secret is in the dry rub and the long cooking time.

Ingredients:

  • 2 racks of pork ribs
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1/4 cup paprika
  • 1 tablespoon black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • Your favorite BBQ sauce

Instructions:

  1. Preheat your oven to 275°F (135°C).
  2. In a small bowl, mix together the brown sugar, paprika, black pepper, salt, cayenne pepper, garlic powder, and onion powder to create the dry rub.
  3. Remove the membrane from the back of the ribs and apply the dry rub generously to both sides of the ribs.
  4. Wrap the ribs in aluminum foil and place them on a baking sheet.
  5. Bake in the preheated oven for 2.5 to 3 hours, or until the meat is tender and pulls away from the bone easily.
  6. Remove the ribs from the oven and carefully unwrap them. Brush your favorite BBQ sauce on both sides of the ribs.
  7. Optional: For a caramelized finish, place the ribs under the broiler for 3-5 minutes, watching closely to prevent burning.
  8. Let the ribs rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving. Enjoy!
