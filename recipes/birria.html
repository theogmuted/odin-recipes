Birria

This is a traditional Mexican dish made with slow-cooked meat, typically beef or lamb, served in a spicy tomato-based sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 2 lbs beef chuck roast, cut into chunks
  • 4 dried guajillo chilies
  • 2 dried ancho chilies
  • 1 medium onion, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups beef broth
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Tortillas for serving

Instructions:

  1. Remove the stems and seeds from the dried chilies. Toast them in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1-2 minutes until fragrant. Then soak them in hot water for about 15 minutes until softened.
  2. In a blender, combine the soaked chilies, chopped onion, minced garlic, cumin, oregano, and a pinch of salt. Blend until smooth to create the sauce.
  3. In a large pot or Dutch oven, heat a bit of oil over medium-high heat. Brown the beef chunks on all sides, then remove them from the pot and set aside.
  4. Pour the chili sauce into the pot and cook for 2-3 minutes until it thickens slightly. Add the beef broth and bring to a simmer.
  5. Return the browned beef chunks to the pot, cover, and reduce the heat to low. Simmer for 2-3 hours until the meat is tender and easily shreds with a fork.
  6. Once the meat is tender, shred it using two forks. Serve the birria with warm tortillas and enjoy!
